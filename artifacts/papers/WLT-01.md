# Model White Paper: WLT-01 (Investment / Wealth Layering)

**SYNTHETIC DATA.** Every customer, account, and transaction in this document is generated, not real. No real customers, accounts, or investigations are referenced anywhere in this paper.

Generated: 2026-10-17 | Dataset seed: 42 | Simulation window: 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31 (12 months)

**Segment numbering used throughout this paper:** 0 = retail, 1 = mixed, 2 = small_business, 3 = HNW.

---

## Plain-English Summary

Wealth/investment layering looks for money shuffled between a customer's own related investment accounts several times, then wired out of the country - a pattern that obscures where money originally came from before it leaves the bank entirely.

On the test period (the only period used for final reporting, never touched during tuning), this scenario at its chosen threshold detected 1.00 (5/5) of the labeled wealth_layering cases, with a productive rate of 1.00 (5/5) among the alerts it raised.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

**Scenario ID:** WLT-01
**Typology:** wealth_layering
**Logic:** At least min_journal_transfers journal transfers (total volume >= min_journal_volume) between relationships.parquet-declared related investment accounts, followed by an outbound cross-border wire of at least min_outbound_wire, all within a window_days window.

This scenario is one of five rule-based detection scenarios in the AML Scenario Tuning Lab. It flags customer-months, not customers in isolation - a customer can be flagged in one month and not another depending on their trailing-window behaviour.

## 4. Segmentation and Rationale

Customers are segmented once (Phase 3, KMeans on tuning-period-only behaviour) into 4 segments, numbered **0 = retail, 1 = mixed, 2 = small_business, 3 = HNW** throughout this and every other paper. A single global threshold would average away real behavioural differences between segments (e.g. a cash-heavy small business and a quiet retail customer don't share a sensible "normal" cash-deposit range), so thresholds are chosen per segment where there is enough labeled signal to do so - see Section 5.

## 5. Threshold Calibration

**Capacity constraint** (`config/capacity.yaml`): up to 1.0% of a segment's customers reviewable per month (floor 5, ceiling 300 alerts/month).

**Selection methodology:** sweep the grid on the tuning period; among grid points whose tuning-period alert volume fits the segment's capacity, select the point maximizing validation-period detection. A segment falls back to the scenario's pooled threshold instead of its own if it has fewer than 5 labeled tuning-period cases, or zero labeled validation-period cases (not enough signal to make a segment-specific decision).

**Pooled chosen threshold:** capacity 200 alerts/month (constraint satisfied: True).

## 8. Limitations and Assumptions

Copied verbatim from `artifacts/metrics/tuning_WLT-01.json`. This is the project-wide limitations list Phase 5 saves into every scenario's metrics file (not filtered to this scenario alone), so some items below discuss other scenarios - included for completeness since this is a verbatim copy, not an edited excerpt.

1. Month-boundary effects: a typology instance whose burst straddles a month-end can be partially invisible to BOTH adjacent months' trailing-window evaluation (documented case: a rapid_movement instance spanning Aug31-Sep1 in the default dataset; confirmed to also affect FAN-02 - a January ring with 6 senders Jan23-29 and hub forward-outs Feb1-7 is invisible in BOTH January (outflows fall after month-end, excluded) and February (inflows fall before the 30-day lookback start, excluded)). This is a structural cost of point-in-time, no-future-leakage evaluation, not a bug - see scenarios.py.
2. FAN-01/FAN-02's tuning-period detection (0 of 17) looks alarming next to validation (2/17, 7/17) and test (2/11, 9/11), but is fully explained, not a bug: the 2 tuning-period rings are the dataset's worst-case instances - one is the month-boundary case above, the other has only 4 senders. The capacity constraint rules out any threshold loose enough to catch a 4-sender ring (min_hub_in_degree 3-4 produces 355-1046 alerts/month against a 200 cap, driven by the new legitimate pass-through look-alikes), leaving only min_hub_in_degree>=5 as capacity-feasible - which the 4-sender ring can never clear regardless of timing. Validation/test's 3 rings (6-7 senders each) aren't affected by either limitation. Verified directly against every grid point, not just the chosen one - see tuning_FAN-02.json's grid_results.
3. FAN-02's detection-rate numbers rest on only 5 labeled ring instances in the whole dataset. Any recovery ratio (e.g. 'X of 5 rings') should be read as a small, directional count, not a precise rate - with n=5, one more or fewer ring recovered swings the rate by 20 points. See ring_report for the per-ring, per-period breakdown (rings recovered vs. member recall) rather than reading detection_rate alone.
4. STR-01's test-period dip (4/8 vs. validation's 4/4) is a genuine threshold-generalization gap, not drift: the 4 missed test-period instances all have deposits spread across 7-10 days, which the chosen window_days=7 structurally cannot fully capture in any single 7-day slide. window_days=10 and 14 tie window_days=7 on validation (4/4 each, since validation's 4 cases all happened to be tightly spread) and score higher on tuning (23/28 vs 22/28); the tie-break toward minimum alert volume - defensible on its own, but under-informed with only 4 validation cases to discriminate on - picked the narrowest window, which doesn't generalize to wider-spread test-period bursts. A larger validation set would likely have broken the tie differently.

## 9. Ongoing Monitoring Plan

**Current baseline:** 2.38 alerts/month and a 1.00 (19/19) productive rate in the tuning period; 2.0 alerts/month and 1.00 (4/4) in validation.

- **Alert volume drift:** investigate if a future month's volume for this scenario deviates by more than 50% from the 2.38/month tuning-period baseline, or approaches the 200/month capacity ceiling.
- **Productive rate drift:** investigate if the productive rate over a rolling 2-month window falls below half of the validation-period level (1.00 (4/4)).
- **Re-tuning triggers:** re-run Phase 5 tuning (a) on a fixed cadence as new labeled cases accumulate, (b) immediately if a segment's labeled case count crosses the 5-tuning-case / 1-validation-case threshold used for the segment-specific-vs-pooled decision in Section 5, or (c) if either drift condition above persists for two consecutive months.
